Vitopoly Rush 2.0 is a three-round, app-driven campus competition blending real-world mini-games with a modified real-estate conquest board game. Performance and assets carry forward across all rounds, culminating in a high-stakes Power Auction. The most strategic faction wins.

  • Must land on unowned property to buy it
  • Cannot buy if insufficient cash

06

Dice Rules

🎲 BASIC MOVEMENT

1-dice system (6-sided each). Move forward equal to total rolled. Actions triggered based on landing tile.

⚡ DOUBLES RULE

If the team rolls a six (6) → One extra roll. Roll 3 consecutive sixes → Sent directly to Detention. Turn ends immediately.

🔄 EXTRA TURN CAP

Max 2 turns in a row. Extra turn granted by rolling a six OR if a Special Tile explicitly grants it. No stacking beyond 2.

🔍 AUCTION ITEMS
  • All Defaulters' highest-value properties seized by Bank

  • Final Score Formula revealed to all teams
  • Buy, Sell, Mortgage, Loan, Repay, Strategize

  • Live physical auction moderated by Bank Authority
  • Teams bid with in-game cash
  • Winning bidder receives the Power Deed™
  • All Power Deeds executed immediately after auction
  • Moderator updates all changes in the Vitopoly App
